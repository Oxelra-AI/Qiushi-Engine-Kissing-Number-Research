## Mathematical contributions

**25 dimensions:** move the 552-point partner layer, add one point and
replace two individual caps. The public head data remain explicitly
identified inputs; the new motion and integer repairs specify the extension.

**27 dimensions:** retain the public first layer and reselect a
311-owner second layer, replacing 310. The two-layer framework and
Lindow's joint optimization are credited to their sources.

**49–55 dimensions:** extend the public 7,069-line class by eight lines
and select image families independently for the seven target sizes.
Exact union counts determine the gains. The root tails and the published
$P_{48p}$ shell are common mathematical inputs.

[Machine-readable comparisons](additional-dimensions.json) preserve the
numbers, fixed source identities and retrieval date. Numerical comparison
dates are not used as dates of first discovery or first publication.
