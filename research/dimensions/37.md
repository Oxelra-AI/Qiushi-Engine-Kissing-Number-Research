# Dimension 37: composing two improvements without losing compatibility

[中文](37.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared construction](../trajectory/codes.md)

The 498,024-point configuration combines a larger support family with a
512-point signed replacement. The two gains are not independent: the
replacement has to fit the boundary of the newly selected support code.

## First enlarge the supports

Joint exchange produced a 2,843-support family. Its saved final
transition replaces 37 old supports by 40 new ones. The forty
individual blocking sets have 84 incidences but only 37 distinct old
supports, another substantial benefit from sharing deletion costs.
Further support search yielded a 2,845-support family.

With the standard dense and root layers, these two sizes give

$$131072+128\cdot2843+2\cdot37\cdot36=497640,$$
$$131072+128\cdot2845+2\cdot37\cdot36=497896.$$

The larger family was then examined for local signed improvements.
Its extra points did not automatically preserve the same local freedom
as the construction in [dimension 36](36.md).

## A partially free twelve-coordinate region

Among 4,098 triples of supports with pairwise intersection four and
twelve-coordinate union, the search identified a region with 207 of
its 495 eight-subsets free of outside conflicts. In the corresponding
36-dimensional example, all 495 were free. This difference matters:
an unrestricted 576-point replacement cannot simply be transplanted.

The [region census](../data/support-regions.json) identifies the input family
and the selected twelve coordinates. The
[recounting program](../../tools/check_support_regions.py) enumerates all
4,098 regions and recovers 207 as the largest free-support count.

The research retained the Hadamard transfer but adapted its source
family to the actual boundary. A pairing of the twelve coordinates
defines fifteen possible image supports obtained by choosing four of
the six pairs. Twelve of these supports avoid the outside conflicts.
For each allowed image support, there are sixteen transverse source
four-subsets, giving 192 source candidates.

Selecting 32 of those candidates with pairwise intersection at most
two gives $32\cdot16=512$ signed vectors after the Hadamard transfer.
Their supports satisfy the boundary condition and their internal
products satisfy the signed-code bound.

## Combining the two gains

The replacement removes three old 128-point bundles. Therefore

$$497896-3\cdot128+512=\boxed{498024}.$$

Relative to the 2,832-support public comparison, thirteen additional
supports contribute 1,664 points and the local replacement contributes
another 128. The total gain is 1,792.

This composition is the important feature of the research. The
support search increases the global count; the signed construction
then exploits a local region that survives in that improved family.
Neither optimization can ignore the other's geometry.

## Reconstructing the same embedding

The [final support family](../../constructions/codes/data/d37_supports_binary.txt)
and [signed replacement](../../constructions/codes/data/d37_signed_patch.json)
contain the coordinate pairing, 32 source four-subsets and 512 image
vectors. Support strings and integer masks use opposite coordinate
orders; the reconstruction fixes their correspondence before checking
the boundary. With coordinates numbered from one, the map is
$i\mapsto38-i$.

The [replacement checker](../../constructions/codes/verify_replacements.py)
regenerates the vectors and verifies their placement in the final
support family. The earlier
[forty-for-thirty-seven exchange](../../constructions/codes/data/exchanges/exchanges.json)
and its [parent](../../constructions/codes/data/exchanges/d37_parent.txt)
preserve the distinct support-search stage.
