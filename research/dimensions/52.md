# Dimension 52: twelve classes from overlapping images

[中文](52.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared proof](../trajectory/p48.md)

The construction gives $K(52)\ge\boxed{52585772}$. It uses 12 automorphism images of the 7,077-line $P_{48p}$ class and the $D_4$ tail system. The selected head lines have an exact union of 84,874 lines.

## The dimension-specific choice

The nominal capacity is 84924 lines; the exact union loses 50 repetitions. The first-occurrence rule turns an overlapping family into twelve disjoint classes without weakening any within-class inequality. The tail system has 24 points, not twelve: antipodal lines index the classes, while individual roots give the pure-tail layer.

The geometric construction removes two equatorial points per used head line and inserts four caps. It also adds 24 pure-tail points. Hence

$$52416000-2\cdot84874+4\cdot84874+24=\boxed{52585772}.$$

Relative to the fixed public comparison 52,585,516, this increases the count by 256. The new objects are the enlarged head class and its selected image family; the lattice, tail root system and cap-lifting framework are the cited mathematical inputs.
