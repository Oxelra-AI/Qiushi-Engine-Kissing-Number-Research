## The dimension-specific choice

The E7 tail supplies 126 roots on 63 lines in rank seven. The selected images contain 445851 line occurrences but 442507 distinct lines. Assigning each line once loses 3344 repetitions and preserves every class's separation. The larger family amplifies the benefit of the enlarged class while making exact union counting increasingly important.

The geometric construction removes two equatorial points per used head line and inserts four caps. It also adds 126 pure-tail points. Hence

$$52416000-2\cdot442507+4\cdot442507+126=\boxed{53301140}.$$

Relative to the fixed public comparison 53,299,730, this increases the count by 1,410. The new objects are the enlarged head class and its selected image family; the lattice, tail root system and cap-lifting framework are the cited mathematical inputs.
