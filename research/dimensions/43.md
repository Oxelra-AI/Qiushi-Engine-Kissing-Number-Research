# Dimension 43: reading a new subsection from a larger lattice section

[中文](43.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared construction](../trajectory/sections.md)

The 2,553,792-point construction came from information already obtained
about an embedded $E_8$ section. The decisive choice was to ask what
that information said about a $D_5$ subsection and its orthogonal
complement, rather than treat each target dimension separately.

## What must be counted

An extremal even unimodular lattice in dimension 48 has minimum
squared norm six and 52,416,000 minimal vectors. The minimal vectors
orthogonal to an embedded rank-five section lie in a 43-dimensional
space and form a kissing configuration after normalization.

The problem is therefore to find an embedding with a large orthogonal
shell. Knowing the section's abstract root type or determinant does
not determine that count: different embeddings of the same $D_5$
can meet the mother shell differently.

## Learning from the parent section

The investigation constructed an embedded $E_8$ parent section and
enumerated its possible inner-product signatures. The mother shell's
spherical-design moments give exact equations for their multiplicities.
The signature domain contains 26,641 possibilities, including 240
exceptional root signatures; these exceptional signatures have to be
included in the moment calculation.

The $E_7$ and $E_6$ subsections reproduced known counts. Their lack of a
numerical improvement did not make the parent calculation uninformative.
The $D_5$ subsection has an $A_3$ orthogonal complement inside $E_8$,
and the already computed orbit data provided a new candidate count.

The resulting identity is

$$1092000+12\cdot116235+6\cdot9180+24\cdot495+12
=\boxed{2553792}.$$

The coefficients count which signature types lie in the relevant
orthogonal subspace. The final twelve points come from exceptional
root signatures.

## Turning the count into a fixed-embedding proof

The parent symmetry helps organize the calculation, but it must not
be confused with an automorphism of the ambient lattice. To certify
the selected $D_5$ embedding, the calculation uses a subgroup preserving
that target subsection. It reduces the signatures to 43 orbits and
forms a rational moment system

$$Az=b.$$

If $c^Tz$ is the required orthogonal-shell count, the stored rational
vector $w$ satisfies $A^Tw=c$. Therefore every multiplicity vector
consistent with the exact moments has

$$c^Tz=w^Tb=2553792.$$

This certifies the target count without having to determine every
individual fibre or enumerate the whole 52-million-point shell.

## What changed geometrically

The old comparison gives 2,545,056 points. The improvement is 8,736,
although the old and new rank-five sections have the same root type
and determinant. The gain resides in the embedding and its relation
to the ambient minimal shell. This is why searching only by abstract
section invariants can overlook a better kissing configuration.
