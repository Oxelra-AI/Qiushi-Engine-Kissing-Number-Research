# Dimension 50: three disjoint images

[中文](50.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared proof](../trajectory/p48.md)

The construction gives $K(50)\ge\boxed{52458468}$. It uses 3 automorphism images of the 7,077-line $P_{48p}$ class and the $A_2$ tail system. The selected head lines have an exact union of 21,231 lines.

## The dimension-specific choice

The selected images are pairwise disjoint. Their union has exactly three times the class size, attaining the maximum for three copies of this particular class. The 7069-line class had also admitted a disjoint triple; enlarging the class then raises the complete configuration further.

The geometric construction removes two equatorial points per used head line and inserts four caps. It also adds 6 pure-tail points. Hence

$$52416000-2\cdot21231+4\cdot21231+6=\boxed{52458468}.$$

Relative to the fixed public comparison 52,458,418, this increases the count by 50. The new objects are the enlarged head class and its selected image family; the lattice, tail root system and cap-lifting framework are the cited mathematical inputs.
