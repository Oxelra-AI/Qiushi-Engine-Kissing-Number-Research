## The dimension-specific choice

The E6 tail has 72 roots and 36 antipodal lines. It is generated inside the E8 coordinate model by two orthogonality conditions; its rational rank is six. Although the stored roots have eight coordinates, their span adds exactly six dimensions. The union loses 921 repeated occurrences from 36 copies of the enlarged class.

The geometric construction removes two equatorial points per used head line and inserts four caps. It also adds 72 pure-tail points. Hence

$$52416000-2\cdot253851+4\cdot253851+72=\boxed{52923774}.$$

Relative to the fixed public comparison 52,922,906, this increases the count by 868. The new objects are the enlarged head class and its selected image family; the lattice, tail root system and cap-lifting framework are the cited mathematical inputs.
