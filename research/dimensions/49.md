# Dimension 49: enlarging the mother class

[中文](49.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared proof](../trajectory/p48.md)

The construction gives $K(49)\ge\boxed{52430156}$. It uses 1 automorphism image of the 7,077-line $P_{48p}$ class and the $A_1$ tail system. The selected head lines have an exact union of 7,077 lines.

## The dimension-specific choice

Only one class is needed. Recombining images cannot improve this case; the gain comes entirely from the eight new compatible lines. This was the reason to return from family selection to the class-extension problem.

The geometric construction removes two equatorial points per used head line and inserts four caps. It also adds 2 pure-tail points. Hence

$$52416000-2\cdot7077+4\cdot7077+2=\boxed{52430156}.$$

Relative to the fixed public comparison 52,430,140, this increases the count by 16. The new objects are the enlarged head class and its selected image family; the lattice, tail root system and cap-lifting framework are the cited mathematical inputs.

## Data, proof and reconstruction

The [mother class](../../constructions/p48/data/class_p48_7077.npy), used without an additional automorphism, specifies the finite input. The [common derivation](../trajectory/p48.md) covers every pair type; here the class is assigned to the unique $A_1$ tail line.

The [verifier](../../constructions/p48/verify.py) checks the catalogue basis, integer products, isometries, exact unions and tail rank. The [search reconstruction](../../constructions/p48/search.py) also checks the eight-line augmentation and records how repeated lines are distributed.
