# Dimension 53: twenty images selected for their union

[中文](53.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared proof](../trajectory/p48.md)

The construction gives $K(53)\ge\boxed{52698696}$. It uses 20 automorphism images of the 7,077-line $P_{48p}$ class and the $D_5$ tail system. The selected head lines have an exact union of 141,328 lines.

## The dimension-specific choice

With twenty images, counting their sizes separately would overcount 212 lines. The selected family was optimized for this size rather than required to extend the twelve-image witness. The construction depends on the resulting union, not on pair-overlap scores alone.

The geometric construction removes two equatorial points per used head line and inserts four caps. It also adds 40 pure-tail points. Hence

$$52416000-2\cdot141328+4\cdot141328+40=\boxed{52698696}.$$

Relative to the fixed public comparison 52,698,222, this increases the count by 474. The new objects are the enlarged head class and its selected image family; the lattice, tail root system and cap-lifting framework are the cited mathematical inputs.
