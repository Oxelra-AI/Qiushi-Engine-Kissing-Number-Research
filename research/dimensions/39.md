# Dimension 39: improving supports while retaining a dense sign layer

[中文](39.zh-CN.md) · [Research overview](../README.md) · [Shared construction](../trajectory/codes.md)

The 763,668-point construction combines 3,383 weight-eight supports with
a 327,680-word dense sign code. Its research route separates two tasks:
make the large dense layer explicit and compatible, then enlarge the
support family without disturbing it.

## Keeping the layers separate

The ERS count in this dimension is

$$N=|T|+128|\mathcal B|+2964.$$

The dense input is represented by ten cosets of a rank-15 binary kernel.
The kernel has $2^{15}$ words, so ten distinct cosets give 327,680 words.
The within-kernel distance and the 45 pairwise coset-distance minima
control the dense layer; all cross-coset minima are ten. This explicit
representation makes the inherited dense-code input independently
reconstructible.

With that layer fixed, a new support contributes 128 points. The
support search optimizes compatibility among eight-subsets, not the
size of the dense code.

## The last exchange and its shared blockers

The [saved parent](../../constructions/codes/data/exchanges/d39_parent.txt)
has 3,382 supports. The
[final exchange](../../constructions/codes/data/exchanges/exchanges.json)
selects fifteen new supports and deletes fourteen old ones. Thirteen
new supports have two blockers, one has a single blocker and one has
three. Thus the separately counted cost is thirty, whereas the union
contains only fourteen old supports.

All fifteen candidates have an old conflict; none alone gives a
positive replacement. Together they are internally compatible and
share enough blockers to give

$$3382-14+15=3383.$$

The exchange is one reconstructible step in the support search.
Against the 3,324-support comparison, the final family has 59 additional
supports. Its full contribution is $59\cdot128=7552$ points.

## The new configuration

Using the exact dense-code representation and the enlarged support
family gives

$$327680+128\cdot3383+2964=\boxed{763668}.$$

The support improvement is the new point-count contribution. The
code-lineage reconstruction identifies the classical dense input and
its shortening relations; it does not reassign that input's authorship.

## Why the decomposition matters

A search over all 763,668 points would obscure where growth was
possible. The decomposition isolates a smaller finite object whose
improvement leaves the large dense layer intact. At the same time,
retaining exact generators and all cross-coset checks prevents a
support improvement from silently relying on an unverified dense layer.
