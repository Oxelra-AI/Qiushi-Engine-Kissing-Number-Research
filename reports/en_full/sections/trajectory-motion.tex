\subsection{Moving the retained shell}
The 25-dimensional investigation changes the geometry of retained points,
rather than restricting every improvement to the original head pool.
A complete block of deleted Leech vectors has retained partners on the
opposite latitude. Moving all partners together preserves their internal
products. Their horizontal coordinates stay on segments between pairs of
original vectors, so convexity settles compatibility with the unchanged
equator. Two exceptional caps are then replaced by normalized integer
vectors. The analytic motion and finite repairs have separate, short
verification arguments.
